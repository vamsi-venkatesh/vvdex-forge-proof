## How the three categories are decided

* **VVDex proprietary** — VVDex authored it. Published for viewing, evaluation
  and verification; no licence granted.
* **third-party (MIT upstream)** — reproduced from a public repository
  under that repository's own licence, which travels with it in
  `exams/public.swe.martinblech-xmltodict-issue-257/LICENSE`.
* **generated public proof** — produced by the publication pipeline from sealed
  evaluation records: reports, digests, receipts and summaries. VVDex
  proprietary, published as evidence.

being here. The only permissive licence present is the upstream project's own,
on the upstream project's own material.
